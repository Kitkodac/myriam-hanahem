ABOUT

MYRIAM HANAHEM

COSTUME DESIGNER

Myriam Hanahem is a Montréal-based costume designer working in film and television. Her work is driven by character, realism and a deep attention to detail. From independent features to international productions, she creates costumes that support the story and reveal the human being behind the role.
